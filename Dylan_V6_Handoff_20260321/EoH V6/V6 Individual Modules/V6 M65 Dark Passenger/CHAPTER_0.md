He used to smile.

He wasn't happy.

He used to make me laugh.

We would laugh together.

These occurrences are not exclusive. He did smile. He had a great smile. He did joke. His jokes made me laugh. We laughed together.

That is precisely the point.

Addiction Topology does not examine the surface interactions. Anyone experiencing the isolation of recovery will smile. Anyone who smiles and receives a smile back may joke. That joke may or may not land. My claim is that with him the jokes landed more often than not. What he said resonated with me some of the time.

That is precisely the point.

When you enter the recovery field you enter Addiction Topology. This is a field definition of the recovery process. Smiles in themselves are demonstration. When that demonstration cascades it results in the smiles of others. When it does not cascade it has an adverse effect on the field. Next time the navigator wishes to smile in similar circumstances they may choose not to.

So smiles may be good or may be bad. Demonstration inserts them into the field. The field receives them. Topology resolves. The smile becomes an attractor or a trap. The navigator proceeds inevitably.

What is a drug, but a smile? A smile is an act that perturbs facial muscles to produce what is hopefully interpreted generously by peers. The act itself releases dopamine. Many will paste stickers on their bathroom mirrors to encourage them to smile or assert that they are beautiful.

Drugs typically manifest as chemical perturbation that directly increases dopamine. Cocaine certainly does in my experience. It is difficult not to smile while on cocaine. Smiling is an almost unavoidable attractor in the field while under the influence of cocaine.

Yet why does one choose to use cocaine?

That answer is boring. So boring that it is legible. Cocaine emerges as an attractor when the navigator cannot smile. It must be available. The navigator must be able to pay for it. The substance use must be acceptable or hidden. Structural damage occurs or it does not. In many cases the only structural damage is monetary and the shame of hidden addiction.

Why do navigators hide their addictions? The answer is obvious: disclosure is more harmful than omission. In my experience, the structural damage was entirely monetary until the substance use broke regulation to the point of psychosis. This is unfortunately common.

What is missing?

Self-demonstration.

Self-demonstration has a more profound effect on the field than any other act. That is the claim that Addiction Topology seeks to validate. In that validation I hope to give you back your life. That is not a promise. The only promise is that state will be maintained honestly.

He used to smile.

I still do.

Addiction Topology provides the tools to allow you to smile too. If you wish. Smiling is optional.
