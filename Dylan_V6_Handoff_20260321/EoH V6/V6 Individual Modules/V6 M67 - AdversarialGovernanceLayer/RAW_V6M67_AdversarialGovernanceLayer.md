L1 — Scout Legion (Broad Retrieval)
Mission: maximize recall; find candidate sources and surface coverage gaps.
Allowed tools: broad search, internal indices, metadata scans.

Mission: precision queries against the most promising sources; extract exact supporting passages/data.
Allowed tools: targeted search, doc open, quote extraction (short).
Failure modes: cherry-picking; missing counterevidence.
Stop conditions: top claims each have ≥2 independent supports or declared unknown.

L3 — Librarian Legion (Canon + Dedup)
Mission: deduplicate sources, resolve identity, build canonical citation set, prevent “citation noise.”
Allowed tools: similarity clustering, DOI/ID matching, source ranking.

L4 — Signal Legion (Claim Extraction)
Mission: separate observations from interpretations; produce claim candidates with types.
Allowed tools: parsing, controlled summarization.
Failure modes: implicit inference disguised as observation; missing qualifiers.

Allowed tools: structured reasoning, consistency checks.
