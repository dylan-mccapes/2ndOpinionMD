2ndOpinionMD
Clinical reasoning & guideline sandbox

Structured clinical answers, not vibes.

2ndOpinionMD is a private research stack that combines a medical knowledge graph, guideline-grounded retrieval, and constrained LLMs to surface traceable, cited clinical reasoning rather than black-box suggestions.

Internal prototype · Not a consumer medical product
  • Built on a curated medical knowledge graph: guidelines, ontologies, and open clinical corpora linked into a unified RAG layer.
  • Ethos-of-Health (EoH) planner routes each question into explicit reasoning modules before any retrieval happens.
  • All answers are driven by retrieved evidence and strict epistemic guardrails: no invented citations, no phantom trials, no mystery numbers.

Disclaimer: This environment is for engineering and research. It is not cleared for clinical use, and it is not a substitute for professional medical judgment.
