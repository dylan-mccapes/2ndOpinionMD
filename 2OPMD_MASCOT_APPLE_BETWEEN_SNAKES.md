## The Thesis (one sentence)

> The apple in the middle is only preserved if both heads of the snake maintain focus. That is 2OPMD.

---

## Unpacked

1. **2OPMD = doctors + patients.** Both must engage. The app is not for one or the other in isolation.

2. **The apple = the app / the shared clinical reasoning.** The thing that holds the value. The thing worth preserving.

3. **Neither snake can hold the apple alone.** If either lets go, it falls. If it falls, it rots. The apples on the tree are young and beautiful. The apples on the ground are rotten and decayed.

4. **Focus from both = preservation.** That is 2OPMD.
